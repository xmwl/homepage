About

I'm a Director of Product Design at Sprout Social. I lead a team of designers and turn big, ambiguous strategy into shipped product. This past year that's meant our biggest bets yet: a full rework of the platform's navigation, accessibility work that opened enterprise and public-sector deals, and the design behind our first AI experiences.

I lead people first and stay close to the craft. I grow my designers, I still pull the data and poke at prototypes, and I hold strong opinions loosely so good work keeps moving. If there's one word people use for me, it's connector.

What I'm known for

  • Master connector. Linking people and ideas across the org, often before anyone realizes they needed connecting.
  • Clarity in ambiguity. Creating focus and forward motion when things are messy.
  • Leading with heart. Building trust fast and making spaces where people feel safe enough to do their best work.
  • Systems thinking plus executional rigor. Zooming out to set direction, zooming back in to grind out the unglamorous details.
  • Executive presence and storytelling. Carrying a high-stakes room and representing the strategic POV with strength.

What I care about

  • AI-first design, done thoughtfully. In a real shipping product, not for the sake of it.
  • Accessibility as strategy, not a compliance checkbox.
  • Platform-scale UX, navigation, and the systems and infrastructure underneath.
  • Design leadership that grows people and connects the org.
